# KIOSK Module

Operators find a visit by scanning the receipt QR (the scanner types the link or the code) on the browser desk or the desktop Sessions page. The desk shows:

- how long the car has been inside
- the entry photo and plate crop, so a bad plate read can be checked and corrected
- the amount still due

Payment is refused when the visit has nothing to pay. The same photo, time, and amount appear on the phone page opened from that QR. Cash is confirmed by a signed-in operator. Phone payment uses the mobile path and does not mark a visit paid from a browser redirect alone.

Plate recognition on a connected camera continues when that camera is not on the Live Gates screen. The layout only changes what the operator is watching.
